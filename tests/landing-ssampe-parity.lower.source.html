Frequently asked questions

How is CareerMonke different from LinkedIn or Indeed?

Conventional job boards rely on sponsored placements, which creates huge applicant backlogs and ghost listings. CareerMonke focuses on verified, active openings so you spend your time on roles that are actually hiring.

Do you take a percentage of my compensation if I get hired?

Never. We are not a staffing agency, headhunter, or contingency recruiter. 100% of your compensation, equity offer, and signing bonus stays with you. We simply give you direct, unmediated access to open employer requisitions.

How often are the job feeds and ATS links updated?

Our job listings are refreshed regularly, and expired or closed positions are removed so you don't waste time applying to roles that are no longer open.

Can I filter jobs by remote status, country, and tech stack?

Yes! You can filter requisitions by global worldwide eligibility, timezone requirements, specific tech stacks (e.g., React, Node, Python, AI/ML), and seniority levels to find matches that fit your exact background.

Do I apply directly on the company website?

Yes, 100% of the time. Every job on CareerMonke links directly to the official company careers page or official ATS application form. There are no middleman forms, third-party redirects, or spam filters between you and the hiring team.
